# Practitioner Bios

## Sita Gurung, massage therapist

Sita trained in Swedish and deep tissue massage in Pokhara and later in hot stone therapy in Bangkok. She has 11 years of experience and has led the studio's massage team since it opened. Clients book her for stress relief and for long-standing back and shoulder tension. She offers Swedish Relaxation Massage, Deep Tissue Massage and Himalayan Hot Stone Massage, and speaks Nepali, English and Gurung.

## Dr. Arjun Thapa, physiotherapist

Arjun holds a Doctor of Physiotherapy degree and is registered with the Nepal Health Professional Council. He spent six years with a sports medicine clinic working with national football and athletics squads before joining Lotus in 2021. He specialises in sports injuries, knee and shoulder rehabilitation and lower back pain. He offers Physiotherapy Assessment, Physiotherapy Follow-up and Sports Injury Rehab, and also gives Deep Tissue Massage as part of treatment plans.

## Maya Rai, yoga teacher

Maya is a 500-hour registered yoga teacher trained in Rishikesh, with further training in yoga therapy for back pain. She has taught for eight years and is known for patient, beginner-friendly sessions. She offers Private Yoga Session and Yoga for Back Care, and is also trained in Swedish Relaxation Massage, which she offers on some afternoons.
