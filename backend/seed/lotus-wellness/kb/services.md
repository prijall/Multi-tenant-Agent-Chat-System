# Service Descriptions

Prices and durations are quoted live by the booking assistant and are not listed here.

## Swedish Relaxation Massage

A full-body massage using long, flowing strokes with light to medium pressure. It eases general tension and stress and is a good choice for a first massage. Offered by Sita Gurung and Maya Rai.

## Deep Tissue Massage

Slow, firm pressure that works into deeper layers of muscle to release chronic knots, stiffness in the neck, shoulders and back, and tightness from desk work or training. You may feel sore for a day afterwards. Offered by Sita Gurung and Dr. Arjun Thapa.

## Himalayan Hot Stone Massage

Smooth river stones are heated and placed along the back and used as massage tools, letting heat relax the muscles before pressure is applied. It is our longest massage. Not recommended during pregnancy or for people with diabetes-related nerve damage. Offered by Sita Gurung.

## Physiotherapy Assessment

A first appointment with our physiotherapist for pain, injury or reduced movement. It includes a history, movement tests and a written treatment plan, with exercises to start at home. Everyone starting physiotherapy with us books this first. Offered by Dr. Arjun Thapa.

## Physiotherapy Follow-up

A shorter session to continue a treatment plan from an assessment: hands-on treatment, progress checks and updated exercises. Only available after a Physiotherapy Assessment. Offered by Dr. Arjun Thapa.

## Sports Injury Rehab

A longer, active session for sprains, strains, tendon problems and return-to-sport training after injury. It combines manual therapy with strength and balance work. Bring sports clothes. Offered by Dr. Arjun Thapa.

## Private Yoga Session

One-to-one yoga adapted to your level, from complete beginners to experienced students, focusing on breath, mobility and strength. Mats and props are provided. Offered by Maya Rai.

## Yoga for Back Care

A slow, therapeutic one-to-one session for people with back pain or stiffness, built around gentle mobility, core stability and relaxation. If you are under a physiotherapy plan, Maya can follow it. Offered by Maya Rai.
