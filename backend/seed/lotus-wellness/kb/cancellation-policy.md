# Cancellation and Late Arrival Policy

## Cancelling more than 24 hours ahead

You can cancel any booking free of charge up to 24 hours before its start time.

## Cancelling within 24 hours

If you cancel less than 24 hours before the start time, a late cancellation fee of 50% of the session price applies. The fee is added to your account and paid at your next visit.

## No-shows

If you do not arrive and have not cancelled, the full session price is charged as a no-show fee, payable at your next visit.

## Rescheduling

You can move a booking to another free time free of charge up to 24 hours before its start time. Moving a booking within 24 hours of its start is treated as a late cancellation plus a new booking, so the 50% fee applies. A booking can only be moved to a time that is free for both you and the practitioner.

## Arriving late

Please arrive 10 minutes early for your first visit to complete a short health form. If you arrive late, your session still ends at its scheduled time so the next client is not delayed, and the full price applies. If you are more than 15 minutes late, the practitioner may treat the booking as a no-show.

## Illness and emergencies

If you cancel within 24 hours because of illness or an emergency, tell the front desk. We waive the fee once every six months.

## When we cancel

If the studio has to cancel or move your booking, there is never a charge, and we will offer you the nearest free alternatives.
