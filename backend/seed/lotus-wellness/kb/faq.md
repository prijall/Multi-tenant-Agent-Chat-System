# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I book a session?

Ask the booking assistant. Tell it the service you want and when suits you; it finds free times, confirms the service, practitioner, date, time and price, and books once you agree.

## What are your opening hours?

We are open every day with shorter hours on Fridays and Saturdays, and closed on major festivals. Ask the assistant for this week's exact hours and upcoming closures.

## What time zone are your times in?

All times are Nepal time (UTC+05:45).

## Can I choose my practitioner?

Yes. You can ask for a specific practitioner, or let the assistant offer the earliest free time with anyone who provides that service.

## How do I cancel?

Ask the assistant to cancel. Cancelling more than 24 hours before the start is free; within 24 hours a 50% fee applies.

## Can I move my booking?

Yes, to any free time, free of charge up to 24 hours before the start. If the time you want is taken, the assistant offers the nearest free alternatives.

## What if I'm running late?

Your session still ends at its scheduled time and the full price applies. More than 15 minutes late may be treated as a no-show.

## What happens if I miss my appointment?

A no-show is charged the full session price, payable at your next visit.

## Can I book two sessions at the same time?

No. You can't hold two bookings that overlap, but you can book back-to-back sessions, for example a physiotherapy assessment followed by a massage.

## Do I need a physiotherapy assessment before a follow-up?

Yes. Everyone starting physiotherapy with us begins with a Physiotherapy Assessment; follow-ups continue the plan from that assessment.

## Which massage is best for back pain?

Deep Tissue Massage targets chronic knots and stiffness. If the pain came from an injury or has lasted more than a few weeks, start with a Physiotherapy Assessment.

## Is hot stone massage safe during pregnancy?

No, we do not offer hot stone massage during pregnancy. A Swedish Relaxation Massage with lighter pressure is a gentler option; please tell your practitioner you are pregnant.

## I've never done yoga. Can I still book?

Yes. Private Yoga Sessions are adapted to your level, and Maya Rai works with many complete beginners.

## What should I wear or bring?

Comfortable clothes for yoga and physiotherapy, and sports clothes for Sports Injury Rehab. For massage, we provide towels and a robe. Mats and props are provided.

## How do I pay?

You pay at the studio after your session, by cash, card or mobile wallet.

## Is there parking?

Street parking is limited, so we recommend a taxi or ride-share. The studio has a lift and step-free access.

## Do you offer group classes or gift vouchers?

Not at the moment. All our sessions are one-to-one, and gift vouchers are not yet available.

## Are you open on festivals?

We close for major festivals such as Tihar, and on Christmas Day and New Year's Day. The assistant will not offer times on closure days.
