# About Lotus Wellness Studio

## Who we are

Lotus Wellness Studio opened in 2019 in Sanepa, Lalitpur. We bring massage therapy, physiotherapy and yoga under one roof so that clients recovering from injury, managing pain or simply looking after themselves can work with practitioners who talk to each other.

## Our approach

Every first visit starts with a short conversation about your health, goals and any injuries. Our practitioners share notes (with your consent), so a physiotherapy plan can be supported by massage and yoga sessions that fit it.

## Location and access

The studio is on the first floor of the Lotus Building, Sanepa Chowk, Lalitpur, with a lift and step-free access. Street parking is limited, so we recommend arriving by taxi or ride-share. There are changing rooms and showers on site.

## Opening hours

We are open seven days a week with shorter hours on Fridays and Saturdays, and we close on major festivals. The booking assistant always checks the current weekly hours and upcoming closures before offering a time. All times are Nepal time (UTC+05:45).

## Payment

You pay at the studio after your session, by cash, card or mobile wallet. Prices and session lengths can change, so the booking assistant always quotes them live.

## Contact

The quickest way to book, move or cancel a session is this chat assistant. For anything else, the front desk can be reached during opening hours.
