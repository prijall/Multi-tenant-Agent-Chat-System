# Returns and Refund Policy

## Unopened tea

You can return unopened tea in its original sealed packaging within 14 days of delivery. Contact us through the chat first so we can arrange a courier pickup in the Kathmandu valley, or tell you where to send it from outside the valley.

## Opened tea

For food-safety reasons we cannot accept returns of opened tea, unless the tea is faulty (for example damp, mouldy or not the tea you ordered). Report a faulty tea within 7 days of delivery with a photo and we will replace it or refund it.

## Damaged teaware

If teaware arrives broken or chipped, report it within 48 hours of delivery with a photo of the item and the packaging. We will send a replacement or a full refund, and you do not need to return the broken item.

## Teaware you changed your mind about

Unused teaware in its original box can be returned within 14 days of delivery. The return delivery charge is paid by the customer.

## How refunds are paid

Because we take cash on delivery, refunds are paid by bank transfer or as store credit, whichever you prefer. Refunds are processed within 7 business days of us receiving the returned item, or of approving a damage or fault claim. Original delivery charges are refunded only when the item was damaged, faulty or wrong.

## Cancelled orders

A pending order that you cancel has not been paid, so no refund is needed.
