# Shipping Policy

## Where we deliver

We deliver anywhere in Nepal that our courier partners reach. We do not ship internationally.

## Delivery times

Orders inside the Kathmandu valley (Kathmandu, Lalitpur, Bhaktapur) arrive in 1 to 2 business days after the order is confirmed. Orders outside the valley arrive in 3 to 6 business days. Remote hill districts can take up to 10 business days during the monsoon.

## Delivery charges

Delivery inside the Kathmandu valley costs NPR 150. Delivery outside the valley costs NPR 300. Delivery is free on any order of NPR 3,000 or more before delivery charges.

## Order life cycle

Every order moves through four stages: pending, confirmed, shipped and delivered. A new order is pending until our team confirms it, usually within two business hours. Once confirmed, it is packed and handed to the courier, which marks it shipped. It is marked delivered when the courier hands it over.

## Changing or cancelling an order

You can change the items, quantities or delivery address of an order, or cancel it, only while it is still pending. Once an order is confirmed it is already being packed, so it can no longer be changed or cancelled; after delivery you can use our returns policy instead.

## Tracking

You can ask the chat assistant for the status of any of your orders at any time. It shows the current stage and when each stage was reached.
