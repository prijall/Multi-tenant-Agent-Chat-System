# Brewing Guides

All guides are for one 250 ml cup. Use fresh, filtered water and never re-boil water that has already boiled.

## Black tea

Use 2.5 g (about one heaped teaspoon) per cup. Pour water just off the boil, around 95 °C. Steep for 3 to 4 minutes; longer makes it stronger and more bitter. This works for Himalayan Black Tea, Rose Black Tea and Smoked Himalayan Tea.

## Golden tips and Darjeeling

Use 2.5 g per cup at 90 °C and steep for 3 minutes. These teas are delicate, so drink them without milk and do not over-steep. Ilam Golden Tips can be steeped a second time for 4 minutes.

## Green tea

Use 2 g per cup at 75 to 80 °C, which is boiled water left to cool for about 3 minutes. Steep for 2 minutes. Water that is too hot makes green tea bitter. This works for Jun Chiyabari Green, Jasmine Green Tea and Mint Green Tea.

## White tea

Use 3 g per cup, because the buds are light and bulky. Brew at 80 °C for 4 to 5 minutes. Silver Needle can be steeped three times, adding a minute each time.

## Oolong

Use 3 g per cup at 90 °C. Rinse the leaves with hot water for 5 seconds and discard it, then steep for 2 to 3 minutes. Steep the same leaves three or four times, adding 30 seconds each time.

## Masala chiya

For two cups: bring 250 ml water and 250 ml milk to a boil with 2 teaspoons of Masala Chiya Blend and sugar to taste. Simmer for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring, then strain into cups.

## Herbal infusions

Use 2 g (or a generous teaspoon) per cup with fully boiling water and steep for 5 to 7 minutes, covered to keep the aroma in. Herbal infusions do not turn bitter, so they can steep longer.

## Iced tea

Brew any tea at double strength, then pour it over a glass full of ice. Lemongrass Ginger and Jasmine Green Tea work especially well. For cold brew, steep 8 g of tea in 1 litre of cold water in the fridge for 8 to 12 hours.
