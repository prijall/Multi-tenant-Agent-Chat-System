# About Himali Tea House

## Our story

Himali Tea House was founded in 2016 in Lalitpur by a family that has grown tea in the hills of Ilam for three generations. We started as a single stall at the Patan Saturday market and now sell online across Nepal.

## Where our tea comes from

Most of our teas come from small estates and cooperatives in Ilam, Dhankuta and Panchthar in eastern Nepal, grown between 1,200 and 2,200 metres. Our Darjeeling first flush is sourced from a partner estate just across the border. Herbal infusions such as tulsi, lemongrass and chamomile come from organic farms in the Kathmandu valley and Dhading.

## What we sell

We sell loose-leaf black, green, white, oolong, spiced and herbal teas, plus teaware: pots, strainers, tasting cups, storage tins and brewing scales. We do not sell tea bags.

## How to reach us

Our shop and packing room is at Kupondole, Lalitpur, open Sunday to Friday, 10:00 to 18:00 Nepal time. Customer support answers chat and email within one business day. The fastest way to get help with an order is this chat.
