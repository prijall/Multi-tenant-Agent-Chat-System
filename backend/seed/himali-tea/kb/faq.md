# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you sell tea bags?

No. We only sell loose-leaf tea, because whole leaves keep their flavour far better. A bamboo strainer or a glass tea pot with an infuser makes loose tea just as easy as a bag.

## How long does delivery take?

Inside the Kathmandu valley, 1 to 2 business days after your order is confirmed. Outside the valley, 3 to 6 business days, and up to 10 for remote hill districts during the monsoon.

## How much is delivery?

NPR 150 inside the Kathmandu valley and NPR 300 outside it. Delivery is free on orders of NPR 3,000 or more.

## Do you ship outside Nepal?

No, we deliver within Nepal only.

## How can I pay?

Cash on delivery only, for now.

## Can I change my order after placing it?

Yes, while it is still pending. You can change items, quantities or the delivery address, or cancel it. Once it is confirmed it is being packed and can't be changed.

## Why can't I cancel my order?

Orders can only be cancelled while pending. If your order is confirmed, shipped or delivered, you can return unopened tea or unused teaware within 14 days of delivery instead.

## How do I track my order?

Ask the chat assistant for your order status. It shows the current stage and the time each stage was reached.

## Can I reorder what I bought last time?

Yes. Ask the assistant to repeat your last order. It will rebuild it at today's prices, tell you if anything changed in price or is out of stock, and wait for you to confirm.

## What happens if an item is out of stock?

The assistant tells you how many are available and suggests a similar alternative. We never place an order for more than we have in stock.

## How should I store loose tea?

Keep it in an airtight, opaque container, away from heat, light, moisture and strong smells such as spices or coffee. A tea storage tin is ideal.

## How long does tea stay fresh?

Green and white teas are best within 6 months of opening. Black tea and oolong keep well for about a year. Herbal infusions are best within 6 to 9 months.

## Which tea has the least caffeine?

Our herbal infusions (tulsi, chamomile and lemongrass ginger) contain no caffeine. Among true teas, white tea such as Silver Needle is usually the gentlest.

## Can I return opened tea?

Only if it is faulty, reported within 7 days of delivery with a photo. Otherwise opened tea cannot be returned for food-safety reasons.

## My teapot arrived broken. What do I do?

Send us a photo of the item and the packaging within 48 hours of delivery. We will send a replacement or refund you in full, and you keep the broken pieces.

## How are refunds paid?

By bank transfer or store credit, within 7 business days of receiving the return or approving the claim.

## Do you sell gift boxes?

Not as a ready-made product yet. You can order teas and a tasting cup together, and add a gift note to your delivery address line.

## Are your teas organic?

Our herbal infusions come from certified organic farms. Our other teas come from small estates that farm with minimal chemicals, but not all of them are certified organic.
