# Product Descriptions

Prices and stock change daily, so they are not listed here. The assistant always checks them live.

## Himalayan Black Tea (HT-001)

Our everyday black tea from the Ilam hills. Malty and smooth with a hint of honey, strong enough to take milk and sugar. A good first loose-leaf tea.

## Ilam Golden Tips (HT-002)

A premium black tea made from young buds covered in golden down. Sweet, with notes of cocoa and dried apricot and very little bitterness. Best drunk without milk.

## Jun Chiyabari Green (HT-003)

A pan-fired green tea from a high-altitude garden in Dhankuta. Fresh and grassy with a toasted, nutty finish. Brew it cooler than black tea to avoid bitterness.

## Jasmine Green Tea (HT-004)

Green tea layered with fresh jasmine blossoms over several nights. Floral and fragrant, and good after a heavy meal.

## Silver Needle (HT-005)

A white tea made only from unopened silver buds, dried gently in the sun. Delicate, sweet and soft, with notes of melon and hay. Our lowest-caffeine true tea.

## Himalayan Oolong (HT-006)

A partly oxidised tea, rolled by hand. Between green and black: buttery, with stone fruit and a long sweet finish. One spoonful can be steeped three or four times.

## Masala Chiya Blend (HT-007)

Strong Assam-style black tea blended with cardamom, cinnamon, clove, ginger and black pepper. Made to be boiled with milk and sugar in the Nepali way.

## Tulsi Herbal Infusion (HT-008)

Holy basil leaves from Dhading. Peppery, clove-like and calming. Caffeine-free.

## Chamomile Flowers (HT-009)

Whole chamomile flowers with an apple-like sweetness. A gentle, caffeine-free tea for the evening.

## Lemongrass Ginger (HT-010)

Lemongrass and dried ginger root, bright and warming. Caffeine-free, and popular as an iced tea in summer.

## Rose Black Tea (HT-011)

Himalayan black tea blended with dried rose petals. Softly floral with a malty base; good with or without milk.

## Mint Green Tea (HT-012)

Green tea blended with spearmint. Cooling and refreshing; a lighter alternative to Moroccan mint tea.

## First Flush Darjeeling (HT-013)

Picked in early spring from a partner estate near the Nepal border. Light, bright and muscatel, with a brisk finish. Drink it without milk.

## Smoked Himalayan Tea (HT-014)

Black tea dried over smouldering pine wood, giving a bold, campfire aroma. For people who like Lapsang Souchong; Himalayan Black Tea (HT-001) is the closest unsmoked alternative.

## Glass Tea Pot (TW-001)

A 600 ml heat-resistant borosilicate glass pot with a removable stainless-steel infuser, so you can watch the leaves unfurl. Dishwasher safe, infuser by hand.

## Clay Tea Pot (TW-002)

A 350 ml unglazed clay pot hand-thrown in Thimi, Bhaktapur. The clay absorbs flavour over time, so use it for one type of tea. Rinse with hot water only, never soap.

## Bamboo Tea Strainer (TW-003)

A hand-woven bamboo strainer that sits over a cup or pot. Rinse and air-dry after use.

## Ceramic Tasting Cup (TW-004)

A 120 ml white-glazed cup, sized for tasting and for small, strong brews. The white glaze shows the colour of the tea.

## Tea Storage Tin (TW-005)

An airtight double-lidded steel tin that holds about 100 g of loose tea and keeps out light and moisture.

## Digital Brew Scale (TW-006)

A pocket scale with 0.1 g precision and a built-in timer, for weighing leaves and timing steeps exactly.
